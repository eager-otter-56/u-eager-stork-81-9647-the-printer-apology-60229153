# The Printer Apology

An apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it — one line, 120 characters max.

> Sorry the toner ran out mid-sentence: I spent 6,204 pages printing my own apology drafts and saved none for you.

The printer apologises for the toner running out, then confesses it burned the toner printing 6,204 pages of its own apology drafts and saved none for the office — the apology itself is the offence.
